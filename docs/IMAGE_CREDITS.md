# Image credits and licensing record

Resort photos appear only on the **featured** single-mountain result, and only for resorts listed in
`site/photos.js`. Every entry needs: verified resort identity, verified reuse rights, a locally hosted
optimized file, and a record below. Do not add photos without completing this record. Do not hotlink.
Do not assume a photo is public domain because it came from a government-related website: check the
original publisher's licence statement and the authorship.
